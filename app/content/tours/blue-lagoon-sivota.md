## Blue Lagoon Sivota

A perfect day trip from Kavos to the stunning Blue Lagoon. Our experienced captain will guide you through some of the most beautiful waters in Corfu.

### What to Expect

We depart from Kavos harbour in the morning and head south towards the Blue Lagoon. Along the way, we'll stop at some of the best fishing spots where you can try your luck with all gear provided.

Once we reach the Blue Lagoon, you'll have plenty of time to swim, snorkel, and explore the caves. The water here is incredibly clear with shades of turquoise you have to see to believe.

### Good to Know

- Bring sunscreen, a towel, and a swimsuit
- Suitable for all ages
- No fishing experience needed
