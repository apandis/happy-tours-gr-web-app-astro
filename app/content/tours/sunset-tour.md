## Sunset Tour

The perfect way to end your day in Kavos. Join us for a relaxed evening on the water as the sun sets over the Ionian Sea.

### What to Expect

We set off in the late afternoon and cruise along the coast, stopping at quiet spots for some evening fishing. As the sun begins to set, we'll find the perfect position to watch the sky light up in golden and pink hues.

Enjoy complimentary drinks and light snacks while you take in one of the most beautiful sunsets in Greece.

### Good to Know

- Bring a light jacket for the evening breeze
- Great for couples and groups
- No fishing experience needed
