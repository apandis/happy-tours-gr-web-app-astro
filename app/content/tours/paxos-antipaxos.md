## Paxos & Antipaxos

Our flagship full-day tour takes you to the beautiful islands of Paxos and Antipaxos — two of the most stunning destinations in the Ionian Sea.

### What to Expect

We depart early from Kavos and head to the islands. Along the way, we stop for fishing and our captain will prepare your catch fresh on board for lunch.

On Paxos, you'll see the dramatic blue caves and sea cliffs. Antipaxos is famous for its white sandy beaches and impossibly clear turquoise water — perfect for swimming and snorkeling.

### Good to Know

- Bring sunscreen, a towel, and a swimsuit
- Lunch is included (fresh catch of the day)
- Suitable for all ages
- No fishing experience needed
