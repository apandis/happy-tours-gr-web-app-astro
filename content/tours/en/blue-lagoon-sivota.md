A perfect day trip from Kavos to the clear, bright water of the Blue Lagoon and the surrounding Sivota coastline.

### What to expect

We leave in the morning and cruise south toward some of the most beautiful water in the area. Along the route, there is time for light fishing, swimming, and cave exploration, with the captain guiding the pace of the day.

Once we arrive at the Blue Lagoon, you can enjoy long swim stops, snorkeling, and the kind of turquoise water that makes this route one of our most requested trips.

### Good to know

- Bring sunscreen, a towel, and swimwear.
- The route is suitable for families and first-time visitors.
- No previous fishing experience is required.
