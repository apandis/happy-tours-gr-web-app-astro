The easiest way to end the day in Kavos is from the water. This route is slower, quieter, and built around the atmosphere of the late afternoon sea.

### What to expect

We head out in the late afternoon, stop for some fishing in calm spots, and keep the schedule open enough to enjoy the light changing across the coast. As sunset approaches, the boat settles into the best viewing position we can find.

### Good to know

- Bring a light jacket for the return trip.
- This route is especially popular with couples and small groups.
- No fishing background is needed.
