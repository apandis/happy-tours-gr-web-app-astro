Our longest route is built for guests who want a full island day with scenery, swimming, and time to settle into the rhythm of the sea.

### What to expect

We leave early and make our way toward Paxos and Antipaxos, mixing scenic cruising with swim stops and fishing time. The route takes in dramatic cliffs, cave openings, and some of the clearest water in the Ionian.

Lunch on board makes the day feel easy and complete, especially when the catch of the day becomes part of the experience.

### Good to know

- Bring sunscreen, a towel, and swimwear.
- Lunch is included on this route.
- The tour is suitable for families and groups looking for a full day out.
